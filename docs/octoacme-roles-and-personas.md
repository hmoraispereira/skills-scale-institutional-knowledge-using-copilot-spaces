# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality standards, test strategies, and validation plans across the project lifecycle. They work closely with Developers and Product Managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and are ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates, testing strategies, and test plans
- Review acceptance criteria and align them with real-world user scenarios
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across features and milestones
- Identify quality risks and blockers early in delivery
- Validate that work meets the Definition of Done before release
- Partner with Developers on automation, coverage, and defect triage

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce escaped defects
- Increase confidence in feature readiness and release stability
- Support faster, safer delivery with clear validation standards

### Typical Communication
- QA sign-off checkpoints and sprint validation reviews
- Defect triage with Developers and Product Managers
- Release readiness checklists and quality summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to build testable features and review defects
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm user value and acceptance criteria
- Supports Project Managers by flagging quality risks and readiness gaps

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, technical review, and design guidance. They help shape scalable, maintainable solutions and ensure the delivery team makes sound technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review architectural choices and technical feasibility
- Facilitate technical design discussions and trade-off analysis
- Mentor Developers and support resolution of complex technical issues
- Identify and mitigate technical debt, integration risks, and dependencies
- Ensure solutions align with platform standards, security, and maintainability goals
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and delivery risk

### Goals
- Deliver robust and scalable systems that meet business intent
- Reduce rework and long-term technical debt
- Improve decision quality across the engineering team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Design documentation and implementation guidance
- Cross-team dependency discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Developers implement solutions that are maintainable and resilient
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and sequencing
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery dependencies and risk areas

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic oversight and decision-making authority for major project outcomes. They ensure the initiative remains aligned with business priorities, funding, and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project direction, priorities, and major trade-offs
- Provide funding, staffing, and business support when needed
- Review project health against business goals and milestones
- Resolve escalated blockers that require executive decision-making
- Sponsor communication with senior leadership and key stakeholders
- Support alignment between project outcomes and broader organization goals

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across stakeholders and teams
- Enable timely escalation and decision-making for critical issues

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive status updates
- Decision meetings for scope, timeline, and resourcing trade-offs
- Escalation briefings for major risks or changes in direction

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers and Project Managers with strategic priorities and approvals
- Reviews progress with Project Managers and receives status updates
- Supports Product Managers in balancing customer value with business realities

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations / DevOps Engineers keep delivery and production systems running smoothly. They ensure deployment pipelines, infrastructure, monitoring, and operational readiness support team delivery and release quality.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment automation, environments, and release pipelines
- Build and maintain infrastructure, observability, and service health monitoring
- Coordinate rollback plans, deployment windows, and operational readiness checks
- Support incident response and post-incident learning
- Maintain runbooks, operational standards, and recovery procedures
- Partner with Developers and Security teams to harden systems and improve resilience

### Goals
- Reduce downtime, deployment risk, and operational friction
- Improve delivery speed without sacrificing reliability
- Maintain a strong operational foundation for production systems

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and deployment coordination meetings
- Incident response and postmortems
- Monitoring and service health review discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to support automation and deployment readiness
- Supports Project Managers on release planning and risk mitigation
- Coordinates with Security teams on compliance, controls, and incident response

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Officers ensure project work follows required controls, privacy standards, and secure delivery practices. They help reduce risk while keeping teams aligned with internal and external compliance expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Review system design, data flows, and dependencies for security risks
- Define and monitor compliance requirements relevant to the project
- Support secure development and release practices across the lifecycle
- Coordinate vulnerability triage, risk assessment, and remediation plans
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews when needed
- Provide guidance on data handling, access controls, and approval requirements

### Goals
- Minimize security and compliance risk for the project
- Improve trust in the solution and operational environment
- Embed secure and compliant practices early in delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and compliance assessments
- Risk discussions with Product, Project, and Technical leads
- Security incident updates and remediation tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and Technical Leads on secure implementation patterns
- Partners with Product Managers on risk-aware prioritization and release decisions
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors in escalation and governance decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability, decision paths, and handoffs across initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective activities.
